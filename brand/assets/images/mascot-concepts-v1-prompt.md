# Mascot concepts v1

Mode: built-in image_gen tool. Preview-only concepts copied into project and embedded in branding.html; not approved production mascots.

## Prompt specification used
Professional brand identity mascot exploration board for Spanish-language beginner AI app-building academy LOS CURSOS DE GRIMI, founded by a Peruvian software builder living in Argentina. Three distinct original concepts side by side on pale warm gray, each with a large full-body illustration and two expression thumbnails. Adult audience, witty approachable indie workshop personality, flat editorial cartoon, expressive asymmetry and substantial hand-ink outlines. No generic robot, AI sparkles, gradients, 3D, glossy toys or copied characters.

- CUY: terracotta guinea pig, messy forelock, work apron, modular app tile, sage and apricot palette. Subtle Peruvian association through species only.
- CARPINCHO: long-snouted capybara at a laptop, relaxed and focused, workshop shirt, plum and dusty rose, dry humor. Not a bear.
- GRUMO: invented flexible irregular cobalt clay block with lowercase-g body motif, asymmetric eyes and terracotta hands assembling app tiles. No antenna or screen face.

Text: “Compañeros de taller”; labels “CUY”, “CARPINCHO”, “GRUMO”. Spacious three-column composition with silhouettes recognizable at small size.

## Inspection
All three silhouettes, labels and expression variants present. Grumo is strongly g-shaped rather than a fully separate block; subject to selection and revision. Screenprint texture is more present than flat production SVG would allow. A final vector-friendly simplified version and consistent expression set remain pending direction approval.
